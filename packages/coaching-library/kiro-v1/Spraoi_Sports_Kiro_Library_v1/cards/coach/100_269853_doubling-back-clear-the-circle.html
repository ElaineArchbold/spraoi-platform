Doubling Back - Clear the Circle

This is a fun game to help develop the player’s ability to double on the ball

Equipment

cones, player, sliotar

Setup

Mark out a circle using cones Divide the players into two teams Position one team outside the circle and the other inside The outside players must strike the balls low into the circle Players inside must try to keep the circle clear by doubling on each ball as it arrives Reverse the roles of the teams after a set time

Coaching Points

Divide the players into two teams • Position one team outside the circle and the other inside • The outside players must strike the balls low into the circle • Players inside must try to keep the circle clear by doubling on each ball as it arrives

Progressions

See source variations and progressions; review and rewrite in Spraoi voice before publication.

Regressions

Use a larger ball, shorter distance or stationary start where supported by the source.

Questions to Ask

What helped you perform the doubling back successfully? What could you change on the next repetition?

Safety

Maintain safe spacing, keep hurleys controlled and stop play before retrieving loose equipment.
