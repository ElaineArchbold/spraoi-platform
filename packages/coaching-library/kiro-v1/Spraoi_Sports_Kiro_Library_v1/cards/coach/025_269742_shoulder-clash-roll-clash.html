Equipment

cones, sliotar

Setup

The players pair off, standing in two lines facing the Coach The Coach rolls a First Touch sliotar for each pair in turn to perform the clash The players retrieve the sliotar for the coach and return to the end of the line Alternate sides on repeat attempts

Coaching Points

The Coach rolls a First Touch sliotar for each pair in turn to perform the clash • The players retrieve the sliotar for the coach and return to the end of the line • Alternate sides on repeat attempts • Task - To increase the challenge: Increase the pace of the roll

Regressions

Questions to Ask

Safety
