Equipment

cones, whistle

Setup

Using cones, mark out a playing area of appropriate size for the number and ability of the Players Divide the Players into equal teams The Players may strike the ball on the ground, dribble and block the ball but may not lift the ball or take the ball into the hand Award 3 points for a goal and 2 points for performing the Hook successfully

Coaching Points

Divide the Players into equal teams • The Players may strike the ball on the ground, dribble and block the ball but may not lift the ball or take the ball into the hand • Award 3 points for a goal and 2 points for performing the Hook successfully • Equipment - To begin with, use a larger sliotar, such as the First Touch sliotar, and as the players become more proficient, switch to a smaller sliotar, such as the Quick Touch sliotar

Progressions

See source variations and progressions; review and rewrite in Spraoi voice before publication.

Regressions

Use a larger ball, shorter distance or stationary start where supported by the source.

Questions to Ask

What helped you perform the hook successfully? What could you change on the next repetition?

Safety

Maintain safe spacing, keep hurleys controlled and stop play before retrieving loose equipment.
