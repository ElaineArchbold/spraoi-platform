Equipment

cones, player, sliotar

Setup

Divide the players into pairs; one ball per pair Player A, the tackler, begins slightly behind Player B The Coach throws the ball forward for both players to run to strike the ball, with Player A attempting to flick the ball ahead of Player B Both players try to gain clean possession before returning to the end of line Reverse the roles after each round The players should practice coming from the opponent’s left and right side

Coaching Points

Player A, the tackler, begins slightly behind Player B • The Coach throws the ball forward for both players to run to strike the ball, with Player A attempting to flick the ball ahead of Player B • Both players try to gain clean possession before returning to the end of line • Reverse the roles after each round

Regressions

Safety
